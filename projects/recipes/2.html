All about the recipe

This recipe was made my grandma. It always was one of my dads favorite foods he had as a kid. He still makes this recipe to this day as it reminds him a lot about his mom.

Quote from my dad

This is what my dad said when I asked him why the recipe was important to you, My mom made this a lot for me when I was your age Ryan, it always tasted so good for dinner after a hard day of work at school

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Thick cut bacon 4 strips
Butter 2 tablespoons
Brussel sprouts, halved 1 pound
Onion, chopped 1/2 of a large onion
Salt and freshly ground black pepper A sprinkle
Hot jalapenos 6
Garlic 2 Tbsp
Black truffle oil A drizzle
Parmeson few shakes
Envy apple one
Fried onion pieces A bunch of pieces

Instructions to cook

  1. Cook bacon in a large skillet over medium-high heat until cripsy
  3. Remove to a paper towel-lined plate, then roughly chop
  4. In the same pan with bacon fat, melt butter over high heat
  5. Add onions and Brussels Sprouts and cook, stirring occasionally, until sprouts are golden brown, 8 to 10 minutes
  6. Season with salt and pepper, to taste, and toss bacon back into pan

Tips and tricks

  • Recipe is still very good as leftovers. Just reheat what you want in a bowl on high for one minute
  • Throw in other veggies in small pieces you like like carrots
