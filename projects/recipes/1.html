Ingredients

Ingredient Amount to use
tuttoross crushed tomato 28 oz
Contadino Italian Paste(Roasted Garlic) 6 oz
DelMonte Tomato sauce 8 oz
Ricotta Cheese part skim 48 oz
Ground meat 2 lbs
Rippled edge lasagna noodles 1 lb
Parmesan cheese Sprinkle of it
Parsley Handful
egg one

Insturctions for how to cook

Directions for sauce

  1. Put a little oil, onion, and garlic in a pot
  2. Brown 2lbs. Then put beef in a pot
  3. Pour 2-3 cans crushed tomato (add a little water)
  4. Pour in 2 cans of tomato sauce and 2 cans of paste and a little water
  5. Cook sauce 1 1/2 hours on High (simmer slowly)
  6. Add sausage
  7. Stir occasionally
  8. Add browned meatballs (fry sepratley first in frying pan with a little oil, turn and brown)

Directions for meatballs

  1. Ground meat
  2. Add 1 egg, salt, pepper, parsley
  3. Add breadcrumbs (1/4 cup to make it thick)
  4. Mix it and roll meatballs
  5. Brown in frying pan with little oil

Directions for Ricotta

  1. 48 oz container, dump in bowl
  2. Add egg
  3. Add small handful parsley
  4. Crush 7-8 meatballs with masher (that have cooked in sauce) and add to ricotta

Directions for noodles

  1. Rippled edge lasagna noodles(1 lb)
  2. 6 quarts water in a big pot
  3. Add a little salt to water
  4. Put in one at a time, stir and lift to keep from sticking
  5. Boil for 15 minutes
  6. Let cool
  7. Run cold water

Directions for lasagna

  1. In lasagna pan, put a little gravy on bottom to cover
  2. Sprinkle parmesan cheese
  3. Lay 3 noodles down
  4. Layer ricotta with meat
  5. Spread gravy
  6. Save 3 good noodles for top layer
  7. Small amount of gravy and parmesan on top of the top layer
  8. Bake 45 minutes at 350
